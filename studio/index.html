The Roy Effect Studio

Also called Queue Commander · Operated by The Roy Effect (Rory Ulloa)

The Roy Effect Studio (also called Queue Commander) is The Roy Effect's tool for scheduling and publishing Rory Ulloa's own short videos to his YouTube channel and Facebook Page.

What it does

  • Queue videos: add short videos with their titles, descriptions and publish times to a posting queue.
  • Upload to YouTube: upload and schedule videos on Rory's YouTube channel using the YouTube Data API.
  • Post to Facebook: publish videos and posts to Rory's Facebook Page.
  • Show basic stats: display basic channel, Page and video stats, such as views.

Who operates it

The Roy Effect Studio is operated by The Roy Effect (Rory Ulloa).
